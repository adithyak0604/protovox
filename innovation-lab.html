ARES
FIELD AUTONOMY / 001
ROBOTICS / AUTONOMYCONCEPT / PROPOSED

ARES — Autonomous Search & Reconnaissance Rover

A rugged mobile robotics research platform designed to map unfamiliar indoor spaces, identify obstacles and stream situational information to a remote operator. The initial demonstrator would focus on safe indoor navigation and mapping.

ENGINEERING OBJECTIVES
  • Build a stable differential-drive mobile platform.
  • Combine depth sensing, inertial data and wheel odometry.
  • Develop mapping, obstacle avoidance and teleoperation.
  • Display rover position, sensor status and alerts on a control interface.
PRELIMINARY REQUIREMENTS
ComputeSingle-board computer + ESP32 / STM32 controller
Perception2D LiDAR or ToF depth sensor, camera, IMU
MotionEncoder motors, motor driver, chassis and wheels
Power & safetyProtected battery, regulated rails, fuse and physical E-stop
SoftwareROS 2, mapping/navigation stack, operator dashboard
AGNIEDGE INTELLIGENCE
ENVIRONMENTAL INTELLIGENCE / 002
EDGE AI / CLIMATE SYSTEMSCONCEPT / PROPOSED

AGNI — AI-Powered Environmental Intelligence Station

A modular sensor network for campuses, farms and research sites. It combines local environmental measurements with edge analytics to detect unusual trends and provide context-aware alerts.

ENGINEERING OBJECTIVES
  • Collect calibrated environmental data from modular sensor nodes.
  • Detect abnormal readings and trends using edge or cloud analytics.
  • Visualise live and historical data in a monitoring dashboard.
  • Explore low-power operation and optional solar-assisted supply.
PRELIMINARY REQUIREMENTS
Sensor nodesESP32, temperature/humidity, pressure and air-quality sensors
Use-case sensingSoil-moisture, particulate or light sensors as required
ComputeEdge computer or cloud analytics service
Power & enclosureRegulated supply, weather-resistant enclosure, optional solar
SoftwareTelemetry pipeline, anomaly detection and dashboard
