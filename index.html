06 — FLAGSHIP ENGINEERING / R&D

Two ambitious builds.
One innovation mindset.

Early-stage ProtoVox flagship concepts designed for multidisciplinary development, technical validation and future showcase opportunities. Specifications are preliminary and will evolve through prototyping.

PV / LAB 001 CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT
AUTONOMY / FIELD ROBOTICS

ARES — Autonomous Search & Reconnaissance Rover

A rugged mobile platform concept for mapping unfamiliar indoor environments, detecting obstacles and streaming situational data to a remote operator. Designed as a research demonstrator—not a certified emergency-response device.

ROS 2 / SBCLiDAR or ToFIMU + encodersComputer vision
CORE REQUIREMENTS
  • Mobile chassis, geared motors and encoder feedback
  • Single-board computer with a microcontroller for real-time motor control
  • Depth sensing, IMU and camera; LiDAR as an upgrade
  • Motor driver, protected battery system and emergency stop
  • ROS 2 navigation stack, mapping and operator dashboard
PV / LAB 002 CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT
⌁
EDGE AI / CLIMATE INTELLIGENCE

AGNI — AI-Powered Environmental Intelligence Station

A modular edge-computing station that combines local environmental sensing with predictive analytics to identify changing conditions and generate useful alerts for campuses, farms or research sites.

ESP32 / SBCEnvironmental sensorsEdge analyticsSolar-ready
CORE REQUIREMENTS
  • ESP32 sensor nodes with temperature, humidity, pressure and air-quality modules
  • Soil-moisture or particulate sensing selected for the deployment use case
  • Edge computer or cloud service for trend analysis and anomaly detection
  • Weather-resistant enclosure, regulated power and optional solar charging
  • Data dashboard, calibration workflow and alert logic
